# TRUSTCORE ADVISORY About Eva Page — Founder Profile Blueprint

## Audit summary

The existing page has strong visual restraint, but it reads more like a résumé-led journal spread than a confident founder profile. The refined page should make Eva’s professional identity immediately clear, explain the perspective she brings, and organize credentials, working background, and practical values into a more deliberate narrative.

## Revised visitor journey

| Moment | Visitor needs to understand | Design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Who Eva is and why her experience is relevant | A clear founder-positioning hero and concise professional summary. |
| Perspective | How Eva thinks about hospitality and operations | A founder principle rendered as a focused editorial statement. |
| Experience | The depth and range behind the advisory work | A guided professional timeline with role areas, not an unstructured CV. |
| Credentials | Why the advice is credible and practical | Clearly grouped professional, language, and consulting-format credentials. |
| Conversion | What to do after understanding the founder | A direct route to Approach or Start a Conversation. |

## Content and visual rules

The page will not invent a portrait of Eva. Until a verified professional photograph is supplied, the visual system will use the Trustcore seal, operational imagery, and typographic composition to maintain honesty. Every section will answer a visitor question and avoid decorative repetition.
