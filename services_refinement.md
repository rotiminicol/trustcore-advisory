# TRUSTCORE ADVISORY Services Page — Flagship Conversion Blueprint

## Audit summary

The current Services page is clean but overly text-led. It presents all four offers as equal rows and makes visitors read before they can visually recognise their situation. The refined page will become a **visual service navigator**: a visitor identifies a relevant service from imagery and a short operational prompt, then opens a clear, compact profile without getting lost in dense text.

## Revised visitor journey

| Moment | Visitor needs to understand | Design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | That this is a focused advisory offer, not a generic consultancy menu | Cinematic services hero with an immediate “choose your focus” cue. |
| Selection | Which service is most relevant | Full-width pictorial service navigator with four visual pathway tiles. |
| Understanding | What one chosen service includes and whether it fits | One active service profile at a time: concise promise, best-fit signal, scope, and outcome. |
| Confidence | How this leads to a real engagement | A minimal four-step engagement ribbon and one personal, clear call to action. |
| Conversion | The next action | “Discuss this service” moves straight to Start a Conversation. |

## Visual direction

Each service will use a dedicated generated editorial image, never a recycled stock-style visual. The images will be linked by a Cypress/Limestone/Brass material story and will depict the real behind-the-scenes conditions of the work: a prepared front desk, intentional guest interaction, workplace readiness, and a focused team-working moment. The service navigator will glide through profiles via low-contrast crossfades, gentle image scale, and progress-line motion—never abrupt carousels or decorative noise.
