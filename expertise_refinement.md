# TRUSTCORE ADVISORY Expertise Page — UX Refinement Blueprint

## Audit summary

The existing Expertise page delivers an elegant high-level statement but leaves a practical visitor with three unanswered questions: **which area applies to my situation, what does the work include, and what changes should I expect?** The refined page will preserve the strong editorial atmosphere while adding guidance, scope, and direct decision paths.

## Revised visitor journey

| Moment | Visitor needs to understand | Design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | What the expertise covers | A concise positioning statement and a three-discipline quick guide. |
| Relevance | Which discipline matches their situation | Clear “when this helps” signals for each specialty. |
| Scope | What the advisory work actually addresses | Short, scannable focus-area lists and tangible outcomes. |
| Confidence | How the disciplines work together | A connected-operating-system explanation linking people, procedures, and guest experience. |
| Next step | What to do with a relevant question | A direct route to Services or Start a Conversation. |

## Design and motion rules

Sections will have calmer length and stronger internal hierarchy. Editorial transitions will remain, but every large display heading is paired with explanatory content, scope markers, or a clear next action. Motion uses the shared rise-and-reveal language, with horizontal quick-guide navigation and subtle image treatment reserved for orientation points.
