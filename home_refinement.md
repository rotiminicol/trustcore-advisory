# TRUSTCORE ADVISORY Home Page — UX Refinement Blueprint

## Audit summary

The existing Home page establishes a strong premium mood, but it asks a first-time visitor to interpret too much visual language before receiving a simple answer to three questions: **what TRUSTCORE does, who it helps, and what to do next**. The imagery, typography, and motion will remain, while the experience moves from an editorial showcase to a guided business narrative.

## Revised visitor journey

| Moment | Visitor needs to understand | Design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | What this is and why it matters | Video hero with concise positioning, one primary action, and one supporting action. |
| Orientation | What TRUSTCORE actually improves | Three clear pillars: operations, experience, and readiness. |
| Relevance | Whether the work applies to their business | A practical “where we help” guide with direct links to the dedicated pages. |
| Confidence | How engagement works and who leads it | A concise three-step process and an Eva credibility block. |
| Conversion | What to do next | A calm consultation invitation with a single unambiguous action. |

## Interaction and motion rules

Motion will support comprehension rather than compete with it. The hero video remains subtle and low-key. Section entrances use a single shared rise-and-reveal rhythm; only hierarchy-changing moments such as the three pillars receive richer transitions. Large decorative movement is limited to the hero and final CTA. Every animation remains brief, interruptible, and removed for reduced-motion preferences.

## Content principles

Copy will lead with plain business outcomes and use editorial language as a supporting layer. Headings answer a visitor’s implicit question. Each visual block has one role, one clear action, and enough negative space to be absorbed without becoming empty.
